Featured case study · Research + product engineering

GovOpportunity

GovOpportunity is an independently developed research and decision-support prototype exploring how small organizations can evaluate public-sector opportunities. I designed the research workflow, information architecture, front-end experience, and validation approach using public information only. This case study describes the product method—not customer engagements, contract outcomes, or non-public agency work.

Working approach

Rigor before polish.
Clarity before complexity.

The strongest technical work makes its assumptions visible, connects conclusions to evidence, and tests the path from idea to use.

  1. Discover

    Define the real decision, constraints, stakeholders, and information boundaries.

  2. Structure

    Separate evidence from inference and translate complexity into a useful model.

  3. Build

    Create the smallest credible system, tool, or research product that answers the need.

  4. Validate

    Test behavior, accessibility, failure modes, provenance, and privacy before release.
